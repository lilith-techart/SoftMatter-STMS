## M1-H acceptance — what was actually tested

- 8 of 8 macro candidates were below project micro-contrast floor `0.0010`.
- Dynamic local contact: pressure 0→0.45 and depth 0→0.0293 did **not** produce a convincing visible dent; 141/150 consecutive frame pairs are identical in the audit.
- Damage/Recovery and Fracture/Regeneration MP4s are real Unity frame exports and deterministic in the reported scope; timeline legibility is still partial.
- Formation is `CONCEPT PARTIAL / NOT ACCEPTED` — not a growth system.
- 19 historical collectors ran without compile or shader failures, **but 66 locked rendered files differed**; that regression decision remains unresolved.
- GPU remains **UNMEASURED**; do not infer device fps from sync timings.
- The original M1-H high-fidelity media are staged offline with checksums; the main showcase currently reuses previously published images with truthful labels.
